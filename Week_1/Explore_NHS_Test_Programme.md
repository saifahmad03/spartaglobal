# NHS Test and Trace

> England's Covid-19 testing and contact-tracing service, launched in May 2020 and absorbed into the UK Health Security Agency in 2021. Main sources: the National Audit Office (NAO) and the Public Accounts Committee (PAC).

## Goals

- **Core aim:** break chains of transmission so normal life could resume
- **Political promise:** a "world-beating" system that would avoid further lockdowns
- **Targets:**
  - Recruit 15,000 call handlers and 3,000 clinical staff
  - Return in-person test results within 24 hours
  - Get contacts isolating within 48 hours of a case being found (SAGE advice)
  - Up to 10 million tests a day via "Operation Moonshot" (proposed £100bn)

| Role | Who |

| Announced by | Health Secretary Matt Hancock (April 2020) |

| Contact tracers | Serco, plus subcontractors |
| Call centre | Sitel |

## Why it failed

1. **Missed its main goal:** two national lockdowns followed and cases rose sharply
2. **Too slow:**
   - 24-hour test results fell from 93% (June 2020) to 14% (October 2020)
   - Didn't reach 80% of contacts within 72 hours until January 2021
3. **Poor value for money:**
   - Over-reliance on consultants cost hundreds of millions
   - Labs averaged only 45% use (Nov 2020 – Apr 2021)
   - Only 14% of 691 million lateral flow test results were ever registered

## Impact

| Area | Impact |
|---|---|
| Financial | £37bn budget over two years; £25.7bn spent by June 2022 (est. £29.3bn total) |
| Public health | Failed to prevent two lockdowns; an Excel error in October 2020 left ~16,000 cases unreported and delayed tracing by up to a week |
| Trust | Accusations of cronyism over contracts awarded without open tender |
