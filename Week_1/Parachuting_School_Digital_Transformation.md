# Parachuting School Digital Transformation Programme Activity


# CRM System – Project Initiation

> **Project:** a CRM for a New Zealand parachuting school to manage customer data, track bookings and send reminders.
> **Stage:** initiation. The aim is to agree *why*, *what* and *how* before any code is written.

## Considerations

### Business
- **Problem:** what's wrong with how customers and bookings are managed today?
- **Objectives:** e.g. fewer no-shows, less admin time, more repeat customers
- **Build vs buy:** an off-the-shelf CRM (e.g. HubSpot, Salesforce) may be cheaper and faster than a custom build
- **Budget and timeline:** what's affordable, and are there deadlines such as peak season?

### Stakeholders
- **Owners and management:** sponsor, funding, sign-off
- **Office and admin staff:** day-to-day users
- **Instructors:** need to see their schedules and customer details
- **Customers:** receive the reminders
- **Other project teams:** website/booking and online store

### Scope
- **In scope:** customer records, booking tracking, automated reminders
- **Out of scope:** the website, the online store, payments (other projects)
- **Customer data:** which fields are needed, e.g. contact details, jump history, certifications

### Integration
- **Website booking system:** bookings must flow into the CRM automatically
- **Online store:** decide whether purchase and rental history is shared
- **Reminders:** choose email and/or SMS, and a provider
- **Existing data:** migrate current records from spreadsheets or paper

### Legal and security
- **NZ Privacy Act 2020:** covers how personal data is collected, stored and accessed
- **Marketing consent:** the Unsolicited Electronic Messages Act 2007 requires consent for marketing messages
- **Sensitive data:** medical declarations and waivers need extra protection
- **Access control:** who can see and edit what

### Risks
- Poor data quality during migration
- Dependency on other projects' timelines
- Low staff adoption
- Scope creep
- Developers starting before requirements are agreed

## Deliverables

| Deliverable | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Business case | Justifies the project, compares costs and benefits, records the build-vs-buy decision |
| Project charter (PID) | Sets out objectives, scope, sponsor, budget and high-level timeline |
| Stakeholder register | Lists who is involved and how |
| RACI matrix | Defines who is responsible, accountable, consulted and informed |
| High-level requirements | Core features as user stories, agreed with users |
| Success criteria / KPIs | Measurable targets, e.g. 30% fewer no-shows |
| Risk register | Risks, likelihood, impact and mitigations |
| Integration overview | How the CRM connects to the website, store and reminder provider |
| Data and privacy assessment | Covers what data is held, where it's stored and legal compliance |
| High-level plan | Milestones, phases and dependencies on other projects |
| Communication plan | How and when stakeholders are updated |
